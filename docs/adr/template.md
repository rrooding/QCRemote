## Context

What's the situation that forces a decision? What constraints, options, and tradeoffs are in
play? State it plainly enough that someone with no other context can follow why this came up.

## Decision

What are we actually doing? One clear statement, not a list of options — the options belong
in Context, the choice belongs here.

## Consequences

What gets easier or harder as a result? Include the downsides and things given up, not just
the upside — that's what makes an ADR worth re-reading later instead of just a changelog
entry.
